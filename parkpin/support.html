FAQ

Where is my data stored? Parking records and photos stay on your iPhone. ParkPin has no account and no cloud library.

Why does the app need Location? To save a parking spot and to point you back to it. Automatic pinning, if you use it, requires Always location access.

How do I get back to my car? Open the saved spot and follow the compass heading. You can also pin or find a spot with common Siri phrases.

What is included for free? You can manually pin a floor, bay code, and photo, then walk back with the compass. Some additional features may require a one-time purchase; that list may be updated in the app and on the product page before release.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone? Restore the new device from an encrypted backup made under your Apple ID.
