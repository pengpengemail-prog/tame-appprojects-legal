Privacy Policy — ParkPin

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

  • TAME ParkPin has no account and no custom server that stores your parking records.
  • Parking spots, photos, and related settings are stored locally on your device (and in an App Group so the widget can read them).
  • The app shows no ads, collects no analytics, and does not track you.

What we store

The location, floor, bay code, notes, and photos you save for a parking spot stay on your iPhone. A shared App Group lets the optional widget and extensions read the same local records. This data is included only in your own device backups; it is never sent to us.

Location, camera, and motion

Location is used to pin a parking spot and to guide you back to it. If you enable automatic pinning, the app needs Always location access so it can save a spot when a drive ends. The camera is used only when you choose to attach a photo of the parking area. Motion and barometer data may be used on-device to estimate a floor difference. These signals are not uploaded to a developer server.

Purchases

The one-time purchase, when offered, is processed by Apple StoreKit.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal profiles, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we operate no server that receives your parking history or photos.

Support email

If you email support, your message contains only the details you choose to include. We use it solely to help you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version published with the app.
