Privacy Policy — SunArc

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Summary

  • TAME SunArc operates without user accounts and without tracking servers.
  • Location data is accessed only when you tap "Use Current Location" to calculate local solar azimuth and elevation via the NOAA solar algorithm. Location data never leaves your device.
  • The app has no advertisements and no analytics SDKs.

Location & Compass Data

CoreLocation coordinates (latitude and longitude) and device heading are used solely on-device to position the 3D sun path sphere and window orientations. We do not store or transmit location history.

Local Storage & PDF Export

Property comparison entries, window dimensions, and shadow occlusion parameters are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. Sunlight analysis reports (PDF) are generated on-device.

Purchases & Subscriptions

StoreKit handles all purchases securely. Apple manages transactions without sharing your credit card or billing details with us.
