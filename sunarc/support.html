FAQ

What algorithm does SunArc use? SunArc runs the astronomical NOAA Solar Position Algorithm to compute exact solar azimuth, elevation, sunrise, solar noon, and sunset for any day of the year at any coordinate on Earth.

How does shadow ray-tracing work? Given surrounding building heights, setback distances, and window orientation, SunArc calculates exact solar ingress windows throughout the winter and summer solstices.

What does Pro unlock? Pro unlocks unlimited multi-property side-by-side lighting comparisons, full-year solar trajectory simulation, and high-resolution Sunlight Comparison PDF exports.
