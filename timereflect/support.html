Help & Support

Match your photos
Use the same viewpoint, lens and lighting when possible. Choose stable background details for alignment. Body movement, plants and changing furniture are poor alignment anchors. Review automatic alignment before applying it; manual adjustment remains available.

Keep your work
Journals retain dates, notes and photo views locally. Export a journal archive to keep an editable backup before removing the app. Protect archives: they contain originals and may include private notes and metadata.

Export and share
Free exports have a watermark and a longest edge up to 1080 pixels. Premium removes the watermark and allows up to 3840 pixels, limited by source detail and crop. Comparison clips are 1–3 seconds; story length depends on photo count and timing. Videos have no audio. Story formats include square, portrait and landscape without Premium.

Purchases
Premium is a non-consumable one-time purchase, with no automatic renewal. Use the same Apple Account and Restore Purchases in Settings or Premium. If the price is unavailable, check your connection and tap Retry App Store. A pending purchase unlocks only after Apple approves and the app verifies it.

Permissions
If camera or photo-save access is denied, enable it in iOS Settings. You can still import selected photos using the system picker. Saving or sharing to another service is controlled by that destination.

帮助与支持

照片如何对齐
尽量保持机位、镜头与光照一致，选择稳定的背景细节作为对齐依据。身体动作、生长中的植物和移动的家具不适合作为锚点。自动对齐后先检查再应用，也可以手动调整。

保留记录
日期、笔记及视角保存在本机。删除应用前请导出记录归档，以保留可编辑备份。归档包含原图，也可能包含私人笔记和照片元数据，请妥善保管。

导出与分享
免费导出带水印，最长边最高1080像素。Premium 可去水印并导出最长边最高3840像素，实际尺寸受原图和裁剪限制。双图对比视频为1至3秒；过程影片时长由照片数量和时间设置决定。视频均无音频。过程影片的方形、竖屏及横屏画幅均可免费使用。

购买
Premium 为一次性非消耗购买，无自动续费。在设置或 Premium 页面使用同一 Apple 账户恢复购买。价格不可用时请检查网络并重新加载 App Store。待批准的购买须经 Apple 批准和应用验证后才生效。

权限
相机或保存照片权限被拒绝后，可在系统设置中重新开启。仍可使用系统选择器导入指定照片。保存或分享至其他服务时，由目的地决定其处理方式。
