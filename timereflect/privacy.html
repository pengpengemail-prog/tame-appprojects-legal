Privacy Policy
Effective October 28, 2026

TAME TimeReflect processes photos and stores journals, comparisons, annotations, framing settings and export history on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a photo-upload service, collect analytics, track you, or include advertising SDKs.

Photos and camera
The system photo picker shares only photos you choose. Camera access is used for reference-overlay shooting. Captures are added to a journal only when you confirm. Add-only photo permission saves exports you request; it does not grant access to your entire library. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Local data and sharing
App projects are excluded from device cloud backup. Exported images, videos and project archives leave the app only when you save or share them. Your chosen Photos, Files or sharing service may sync or upload those files under its own policies. Journal archives include original photos and may include embedded photo metadata, dates, view labels and notes. Check the contents and recipient before sharing.

Purchases
Optional Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions to provide access and restore purchases. We do not receive payment-card details. Apple's services may require a network connection and process purchase information under Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and control
Local data remains until removed by available app controls or the app is deleted. Comparisons in Recently Deleted remain available for restoration. Removing this app removes its local container; it does not delete originals or exports in Photos, Files, shared destinations or backups you created. Export a journal archive before uninstalling if you want to keep an editable copy.

Support and changes
The in-app Help center explains alignment, exports, backups and purchase recovery. If you contact support, share only details you choose and avoid sending private photos. The current bundled policy is available offline in Settings and on the Premium screen. For privacy questions, email pengpengemail@gmail.com. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy.

隐私政策
生效日期：2026年10月28日

TAME TimeReflect 在设备本机处理照片并保存记录、对比工程、标注、取景设置和导出历史。无需注册账号。我们不提供照片上传服务，不接入分析统计、用户追踪或广告 SDK。

照片与相机
系统照片选择器仅向本应用提供你选取的照片。相机用于参考图叠加取景，拍摄后由你确认才加入记录。仅添加照片权限用于保存你主动导出的内容，不允许读取整个相册。可在系统设置中管理权限。

本地数据与分享
应用工程不参与设备云备份。仅当你主动保存或分享时，导出图片、视频和工程归档才会离开应用。你选择的相册、文件位置或分享服务可能依其规则同步或上传这些文件。记录归档包含原始照片，也可能包含照片原有元数据、日期、视角名称和笔记，分享前请确认文件内容和接收者。

购买
可选 Premium 通过 Apple 进行一次性买断。应用验证 StoreKit 交易以提供权益与恢复购买，我们不获取银行卡信息。Apple 服务可能需要联网，并依其隐私政策处理购买信息。

保存与控制
本机数据会保留到你使用现有管理功能移除，或删除应用为止。最近删除中的对比工程仍可恢复。删除应用会移除其本地容器，但不会删除系统相册、文件、分享目的地或自行创建的备份中的原图和导出内容。如需保留可编辑记录，请在卸载前导出记录归档。

帮助与更新
应用内帮助中心说明对齐、导出、备份与恢复购买。联系支持时仅提供你愿意分享的信息，避免发送私人照片。本政策可在设置和 Premium 页面离线阅读。隐私问题可发邮件至 pengpengemail@gmail.com。重要变更将更新本政策。
