English

For help with Levain Log, email pengpengemail@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include payment-card information or private records unless necessary.

Purchase and restore

Pro is an optional non-consumable purchase. Before purchase the app displays the price supplied by the App Store. If the price cannot be loaded, use Try again. To restore, use Restore purchase with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

Your records

In Settings, Export backup saves a file containing your records and included photos. Keep a separate copy before importing, because importing replaces the app's current records. The app has no account-based sync service.

购买与恢复

Pro 为可选的一次性非消耗型购买。购买前，App 显示 App Store 提供的价格；加载失败时可重试。恢复购买时，请使用原购买 Apple 账户并点击恢复购买。账单与退款由 Apple 处理。

你的记录

可从设置导出包含记录与随附照片的备份。导入会替换 App 当前记录，请先另存一份现有备份。App 不提供基于账号的同步服务。
