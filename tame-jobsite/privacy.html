TAME Jobsite – Privacy Policy

TAME Jobsite is a calculator, job and quote app for contractors. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

The app does not collect, track, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device. Jobs, materials, quotes, client names, addresses and measurements you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them; the JSON export in the More tab lets you keep a backup.

Exports. PDF quotes and JSON backups are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share or save them.

Purchases. The optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The app contacts the App Store only to show the price, buy, or restore the purchase. We never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Children. The app is a work tool for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated text will appear here and in a new version of the app.
