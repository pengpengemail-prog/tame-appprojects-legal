TAME Jobsite – Support

TAME Jobsite is an offline calculator, job and quote app for contractors.

Restore a purchase. Open the Pro screen (More tab, or when exporting a PDF) and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Back up your jobs. More tab, Export All Jobs (JSON). The app also keeps automatic snapshots from each launch.

Report a problem or a wrong number. Email the calculator name, the values you entered, and the result you expected.
