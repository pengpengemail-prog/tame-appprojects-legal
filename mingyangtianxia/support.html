TAME BrandLab · Support

Updated 2026-11-10. The following applies to the redesigned iOS workspace. Earlier versions may have different features.

Open Brand Studio to generate candidates, open a brand card, save names to the default project, and compare two saved candidates. A domain check reports address records or lookup errors. It does not determine registration availability or trademark clearance.

Name generation, shortlisting in the default project, and comparison are available without a subscription. An active monthly or yearly Pro subscription enables separate naming projects, shortlist export, and brand card image sharing. Open Projects > View Pro plans to see the available plans. Use Restore purchases from the workspace menu or the purchase screen. Apple handles billing, renewal and cancellation. Apple Standard EULA.

在品牌工作台可生成候选、打开品牌名片、在默认项目保存短名单，并并排比较两个候选。域名查询仅报告地址记录或查询错误，不判断可注册性或商标冲突。

生成候选、默认项目的短名单和比较功能无需订阅。有效的月度或年度 Pro 订阅提供独立命名项目、短名单导出及品牌名片图片分享。在「项目」中可查看 Pro 方案；工作台菜单及付费页提供恢复购买。Apple 负责扣费、续订和取消。Apple 标准使用条款。
