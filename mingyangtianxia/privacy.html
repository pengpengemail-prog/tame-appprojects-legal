TAME BrandLab · Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-10. The following applies to the redesigned iOS workspace. Earlier versions may have different features.

The app generates Chinese brand name candidates on your device using the brief and style you choose and a bundled dictionary. Project names and shortlists are stored on your device. The developer does not receive them. The app does not require an account or track you across apps.

When you choose Check domains, the device’s DNS resolver queries address records for the displayed domain names. The DNS provider and network operators may receive the queried domain names. Name generation and comparison do not require domain checks.

If you export a shortlist or share a brand card, the system share sheet passes the selected content to the destination you choose. Apple processes subscription purchases and restoration; the app reads StoreKit entitlements to determine which features are available.

You can remove entries from your shortlist in the app. Removing local app data does not delete copies you shared. Use the support page for privacy questions.

隐私政策

App 根据你输入的关键词、选择的风格和内置字库，在设备上生成中文品牌名候选。项目名称和短名单保存在设备上，开发者不接收这些数据。App 无需注册，也不进行跨 App 跟踪。

你主动选择查询域名时，App 通过设备的 DNS 解析器查询所显示域名的地址记录。DNS 服务提供商和网络运营方可能接收到被查询的域名。生成和比较候选名无需查询域名。

导出短名单或分享品牌名片时，系统分享菜单会将所选内容交给你选择的目标。订阅购买和恢复由 Apple 处理；App 通过 StoreKit 权益判断可用功能。

你可在 App 内移除短名单条目。移除本地 App 数据不会删除已分享的副本。如有隐私问题，请通过支持页联系。
