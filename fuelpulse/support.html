FAQ

Where is my data stored? Fill-up and charge records stay on your iPhone. FuelPulse has no account and no cloud library.

Are fuel and EV mixed together? No. Fuel and EV modes are tracked separately.

What can I do for free? You can log fill-ups or charges and see consumption per 100 km, cost per kilometer, and recent entries. Additional features may require a one-time purchase; that list may be updated in the app and on the product page before release.

Does the camera save or upload my dashboard photo? No. When that feature is available, the frame is used only for on-device recognition to fill in mileage. It is not saved or uploaded by the app.

Is the interface available in English? This version of the app is in Simplified Chinese. English store text, if added later, will wait until the interface is translated.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone? Restore the new device from an encrypted backup made under your Apple ID.
