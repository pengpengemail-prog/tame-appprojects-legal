Privacy Policy — SpeedHUD

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

  • TAME SpeedHUD has no account and no custom server.
  • Speed and trip figures are computed on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • The app shows no ads, collects no analytics, and does not track you.

Location and motion

With your permission, the app uses Location while you use it to compute GPS speed and trip figures on the device. Motion data may also be used on-device. These readings are not sent to a developer server.

What we store

Settings such as units and your chosen speed limit stay in local storage on the device. We do not operate a server that receives your trips.

Purchases

If a purchase is offered, it is processed by Apple StoreKit.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal profiles, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we do not receive a live track of your driving.

Support email

If you email support, your message contains only the details you choose to include. We use it solely to help you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version published with the app.
