FAQ

Is the speed reading official? No. GPS speed is for reference only. Always trust the vehicle's instrument cluster and follow traffic laws.

Should I use the phone while driving? No. Set up the display and place the phone on the dash before you drive. Do not interact with the phone while the vehicle is moving.

Why does the app need Location? To compute speed and trip figures on your device. Location is not uploaded to a developer server.

What is Windshield Mode? It mirrors the display so large numerals can be reflected on the windshield when the phone sits on the dash.

Where is my data stored? Settings stay on your device. SpeedHUD has no account and no cloud log of your trips.
