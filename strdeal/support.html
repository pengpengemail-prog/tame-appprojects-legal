FAQ

What does the app do? TOMI STR Deal analyzes short-term rental deals on your iPhone. You can model a property purchase or a rental-arbitrage deal, then review cash-on-cash return and related investment metrics on one screen.

What is included in the subscription? The subscription unlocks unlimited saved deals, scenario comparison and long-term rental checks, the seasonal occupancy curve, and multi-page PDF reports. Annual and monthly plans are available. There is no lifetime purchase.

Where is my data stored? Deal inputs and results stay on your device. You can export a PDF report when you want to share or keep a copy.
