Spray Foam & Insulation Calc Privacy Policy

Spray Foam & Insulation Calc is an offline calculator for insulation R-value, heat loss, spray-foam kits and loose-fill quantities. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

The app does not collect, track, sell or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your inputs and saved calculations are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
