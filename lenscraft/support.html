FAQ

What LUT formats are supported? LensCraft natively supports standard 3D LUT `.cube` files (size 17x17x17 through 65x65x65) compatible with DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere, and Final Cut Pro.

How does the Luma Scope work? The realtime waveform analyzer computes luminance values from 0 to 100 IRE across horizontal scanlines to monitor clipping and black crush.

What is Halation? LensCraft's film halation filter simulates the photochemical scattering of red wavelengths around high-contrast specular edges, replicating authentic 35mm film emulsions.

What does Pro unlock? Pro unlocks full 4K master image export, unlimited .cube 3D LUT export for desktop NLEs, and access to the complete cinema emulsion library.
