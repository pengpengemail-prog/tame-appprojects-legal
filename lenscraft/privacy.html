Privacy Policy — LensCraft

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Summary

  • TAME LensCraft does not require accounts or logins and runs entirely on your device.
  • Photos and video frames you import are processed locally with CoreImage 3D color cubes and GPU shaders. Nothing is uploaded to remote servers.
  • No personal data, identifiers, or usage telemetry are tracked or shared.

Photos & File Import

Photo library access is used only to pick reference still frames and save graded 4K images. The app does not index or scan your photo library.

LUT (.cube) Processing

Custom 3D LUT (.cube) files you import are parsed and cached in your device's sandboxed storage. Exported LUTs are generated directly on device.

Purchases & Subscriptions

Apple StoreKit processes all transactions. We do not have access to your payment information.
