CarSpecs Support

Need help with CarSpecs? Contact pengpengemail@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, the vehicle you selected (make, model, year, engine) and a short description of the issue.

About the data

  • Specification values come from the vehicle manufacturer's owner's manual; each value shows the PDF page it was taken from so you can check the original.
  • Vehicle and engine-bay images are illustrative renders, not photos of your exact vehicle. Always confirm with your owner's manual or a qualified technician before servicing your vehicle.
  • CarSpecs is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any vehicle manufacturer or parts retailer. Vehicle names are used only to identify compatibility.
  • Found a wrong value? Email us the vehicle and the manual page and we will correct it.

Subscriptions

You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app to restore a previous purchase on a new device.
