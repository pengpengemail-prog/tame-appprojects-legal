CarSpecs Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026.

CarSpecs shows maintenance specifications (such as engine oil, bulbs, spark plugs and tire pressure) for supported vehicles, with the owner's manual page each value comes from.

What the app stores

  • No account is required. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or VIN.
  • Vehicles you save to your garage are stored only on your device. You can remove them at any time by swiping to delete in the Garage tab; deleting the app removes them completely.
  • The app does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Network use

  • The app may download updated vehicle specification files from our static data host. These requests contain no personal information; like any web server, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability.
  • When you open an owner's manual or a web search from the app, the page is loaded from that third-party website (for example the vehicle manufacturer's site or a search engine) and is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks whether you have an active purchase through Apple's StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. For privacy questions, contact pengpengemail@gmail.com.
