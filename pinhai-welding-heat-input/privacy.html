Weld Heat Input Calculator Privacy Policy

Weld Heat Input Calculator is an offline calculator for welding heat input, travel speed for a target heat input, and fillet weld size. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

The app does not collect, track, sell or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your inputs and saved calculations are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
