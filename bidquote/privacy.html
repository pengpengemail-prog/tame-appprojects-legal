Privacy Policy — BidQuote

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Summary

  • TAME BidQuote requires no login and runs entirely on your device.
  • Your client names, project quotes, margin figures, and handwritten signatures are stored strictly in local device sandboxed storage. No proposal data is ever sent to a developer server.
  • No third-party trackers, advertisements, or data brokers are integrated into the app.

Handwritten Signature Data

Signatures drawn via PencilKit are rendered directly onto the generated PDF document and saved in local storage. Signature vectors or images are never uploaded or shared externally.

PDF Export

Proposal and invoice PDFs are created locally on iOS using UIGraphicsPDFRenderer. You have complete control over sharing and exporting.

Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases are securely processed by Apple StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details.
