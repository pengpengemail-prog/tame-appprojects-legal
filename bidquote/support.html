FAQ

How does profit margin calculation work? BidQuote calculates Subtotal, Discount, Net Subtotal, Tax, Total Bid, and automatically evaluates Net Profit and Profit Margin (%) against your defined direct costs.

Can clients sign proposals on-screen? Yes. BidQuote includes a signature block powered by Apple PencilKit, allowing you or your client to sign directly with a finger or Apple Pencil, which embeds cleanly into the bottom of the proposal PDF.

What is included in the Pro tier? The Pro version removes the 3-proposal limit, enables custom business branding/logos on proposals, and unlocks unlimited high-resolution PDF exports with one-tap client sharing.
