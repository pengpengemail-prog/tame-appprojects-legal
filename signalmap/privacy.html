Privacy Policy — SignalMap

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

  • TAME SignalMap has no account and no custom server that stores your maps.
  • Floor plans, sample points, and PDF reports are stored locally on your device.
  • The app shows no ads, collects no analytics, and does not track you.

What we store

Room scans, templates, sample measurements, settings, and any PDF you export stay in the app's local storage (including UserDefaults and Documents). This data is included only in your own device backups; it is never sent to us.

Camera and LiDAR

The camera and LiDAR scanner are used only when you start a room scan with Apple RoomPlan, so the app can build a floor plan on your device. The app does not upload scan results or use the camera to collect photos of people.

Local Network and a latency probe

With your permission, SignalMap uses the local network to measure gateway latency, jitter, and packet loss, discover devices on your LAN, and open your router's admin page in the system browser. These results stay on your device.

If the local gateway cannot be reached, the app may open a short TCP handshake to 1.1.1.1:443 only to measure round-trip time. Floor plans and sample data are not uploaded in that probe. Purchases and restore requests go through Apple StoreKit.

Location

The app does not request Location permission and does not collect location data.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal profiles, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we operate no server that receives your floor plans or samples.

Support email

If you email support, your message contains only the details you choose to include. We use it solely to help you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version published with the app.
