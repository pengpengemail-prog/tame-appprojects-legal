FAQ

Where is my data stored? Floor plans, samples, and reports stay on your device. SignalMap has no account and no cloud server for this content.

Do I need LiDAR? Automatic 3D room scanning needs a device that supports RoomPlan (typically iPhone or iPad models with LiDAR). On other devices you can still sample coverage using the built-in templates.

Is the Mbps number a speed test? No. It is an on-device estimate derived from latency, and the app labels it as an estimate. Coverage grades use gateway latency, jitter, and packet loss, not radio RSSI.

Why does the app ask for Local Network? To measure gateway quality at each sample, discover devices on your LAN, and open the router admin page in the system browser. If the gateway is unreachable, a short handshake to 1.1.1.1:443 is used only to measure round-trip time.

What is free and what is paid? You can take 4 sample points and view the weakest room for free. A paid plan (weekly, yearly with a 3-day trial, or a one-time purchase) unlocks unlimited samples, router placement suggestions, and PDF export.

How do I move my maps to a new phone? Restore the new device from an encrypted backup made under your Apple ID. You can also export a PDF if you have unlocked that feature.
