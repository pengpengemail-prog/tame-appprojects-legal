Privacy Policy — SoundProof

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Summary

  • TAME SoundProof has no account and no cloud server that collects or stores your audio.
  • Audio processed by the decibel meter is analyzed in real-time in memory using Apple's Accelerate framework and discarded immediately. No raw audio recordings are sent off your device.
  • The app contains no ads, uses no third-party tracking SDKs, and respects your privacy.

Microphone Usage

Microphone access is required solely to measure ambient sound pressure levels (dB) and calculate 1/3-octave frequency spectrums in real time. The raw audio is never recorded, transmitted, or stored on external servers.

Local Storage & Evidence Export

Noise incident snapshots and Sabine RT60 acoustic estimates are stored entirely in local device storage. Exported PDF reports are generated locally via iOS graphics renderers and only shared when you choose to share them.

Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Apple StoreKit. We do not process or store any financial or payment credentials.
