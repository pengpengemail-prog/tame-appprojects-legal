FAQ

How accurate is the decibel measurement? TAME SoundProof utilizes calibrated hardware microphones with built-in A-weighting (dBA) and C-weighting filters. You can also fine-tune the calibration offset (±10 dB) in Settings against a reference sound meter.

Does the app save audio recordings? No. SoundProof computes real-time FFT frequency bands in device memory and immediately discards audio buffers. Only numerical decibel logs and spectrum peak values are saved.

What is Sabine RT60 calculation? It calculates reverberation decay time based on room dimensions (length, width, height) and absorption coefficients for acoustic treatment planning.

How do subscriptions and unlocks work? TAME SoundProof offers optional Pro access (Weekly, Yearly with 3-day trial, or Lifetime). Pro unlocks unlimited noise dispute evidence PDF exports and full 24-band 1/3-octave spectrum history.
