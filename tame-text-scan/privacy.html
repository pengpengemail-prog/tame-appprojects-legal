TAME Text Scan Privacy Policy

TAME Text Scan is a local OCR utility. It does not require an account, does not track users, and does not sell or share personal data.

Camera images and selected photos are used only for on-device text recognition. Recognized text history is stored locally.

If you purchase TAME Text Scan Pro, Apple processes the transaction. The app uses StoreKit only to request product information, complete or restore the purchase, and verify the resulting entitlement. TAME Text Scan does not receive your full payment-card details.

You can remove locally stored scan history by deleting it in the app or uninstalling the app.
