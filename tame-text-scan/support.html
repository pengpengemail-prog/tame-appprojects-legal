TAME Text Scan Support

TAME Text Scan performs OCR on your device and stores scan history locally. No account is required.

Purchases

TAME Text Scan Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. To restore a previous purchase, open Settings in the app, choose “Buy TAME Pro once,” then tap “Restore Purchases.” Purchases and refunds are processed by Apple.
