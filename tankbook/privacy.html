English

Tankbook requires no app account. Its current version has no advertising, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated data server. The developer does not receive your aquariums, water tests, inhabitants, maintenance, equipment and photos through normal app use.

Records, photos and storage

Your aquariums, water tests, inhabitants, maintenance, equipment and photos are stored in the app's local storage. Photo access uses the system picker to access items you choose; it does not grant the developer access to your photo library. Apple Photos may retrieve a selected item from iCloud according to your device settings.

You can delete individual records using the app. Removing the app removes its local storage, subject to iOS backup and device settings. Local snapshots and exported backups may retain earlier copies; deleting a record does not erase copies that you have already exported. Keep an export before moving devices or deleting the app.

Permissions and Apple services

The app may request notifications for maintenance reminders. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Reminders are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Notifications are not a remote messaging service operated by the developer.

Price lookup, optional Pro purchase and purchase restoration use Apple StoreKit and may require internet access. Apple handles payment and Apple Account data; the developer does not operate a receipt-validation server or receive your payment-card details. Device backup and any cloud provider you choose are governed by their own settings and policies.

Exports and support

Exporting or sharing a backup happens at your request. Backups contain your records and included photos, so keep them in a location you trust. The destination you choose may upload the file to its cloud service. If you contact support, the developer receives the contents and address of the email you send. Send only information needed to explain the problem.

中文

缸志无需注册 App 账号。当前版本没有广告、第三方分析 SDK 或开发者数据服务器。正常使用时，开发者不会收到你的鱼缸、水质检测、生物、养护、设备及照片。

记录、照片与存储

你的鱼缸、水质检测、生物、养护、设备及照片存放在 App 本地存储中。照片通过系统选择器读取你主动选中的项目；开发者不会因此获得照片图库访问权限。根据设备设置，Apple 照片服务可能从 iCloud 获取所选项目。

你可以在 App 中删除单条记录。卸载 App 会移除本地存储，但仍受 iOS 备份与设备设置影响。本地快照及导出的备份可能包含早期副本，删除记录不会同步删除已导出副本。换设备或卸载前，请先保存备份。

权限与 Apple 服务

App 可能请求用于养护提醒的通知。你可在 iOS 设置中更改权限。

提醒在你允许通知后由本机安排，你可在 iOS 设置中关闭。开发者不运营远程推送消息服务。

价格查询、可选 Pro 购买与恢复购买使用 Apple StoreKit，可能需要联网。Apple 处理支付和 Apple 账户信息，开发者不运营收据验证服务器，也不会收到银行卡信息。设备备份及你选择的云服务受各自设置与政策管理。

导出与支持

备份导出或分享由你主动发起。备份包含记录及随附照片，请妥善保管；所选存储位置可能通过其云服务上传文件。若发送支持邮件，开发者会收到邮件内容及发件地址，请只提供排查所需信息。
