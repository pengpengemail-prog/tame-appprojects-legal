Drive Log Privacy Policy

Drive Log is an offline app for learner drivers and their supervising adults to record practice drives, track skills and review state requirements. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

The app does not collect, track, sell or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not use location services. The drives, names, notes and hand-drawn supervisor signatures you enter are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud or device backups if you turn those on in iOS). Deleting the app deletes them. When you export a backup or a PDF log, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose.

The app has no social features and no user-generated content shared with others. It is intended for learner drivers and the adults who supervise them.
