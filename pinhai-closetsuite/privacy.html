Closet Suite Privacy Policy

Closet Suite is an offline app for your wardrobe, outfit planning and wear tracking. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

The app does not collect, track, sell or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Photos, notes and records you add are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud or backups if you turn those on in iOS). Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photo library access is used only when you choose to add a photo, and the photo is processed on your device; nothing is uploaded.

The app is suitable for ages 13 and older and contains no social features or user-generated content shared with others.
