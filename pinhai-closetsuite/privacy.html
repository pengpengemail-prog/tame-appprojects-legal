Closet Suite Privacy Policy

Closet Suite is a private wardrobe, outfit-planning and wear-tracking app. No account is required. Your clothing photos, notes, wardrobe records and saved outfits stay on your device. Background removal, colour extraction, garment classification and outfit calculations run on the device.

We do not operate a server that receives your wardrobe or photos. The app has no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking. iOS backups that you choose to use may include local app data. An exported Closet Suite records file does not contain the photo files.

Optional city search and weather features need an internet connection. City searches are processed by Apple MapKit, and forecasts are requested from Apple WeatherKit using the selected city's geographic coordinates. Clothing photos, wardrobe records and notes are not included in those requests. Apple may also provide the weather attribution artwork and legal information shown in the app. These Apple services are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photo-library access is used when you choose to add a clothing photo. The selected image is processed locally and is not uploaded by Closet Suite. You can remove records and photos in the app; uninstalling removes local app data, while any backups you created are managed separately.

The app does not share user content with other users.
