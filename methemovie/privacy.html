Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

ME: THE MOVIE (主演：我) · Updated September 24, 2026

English

ME: THE MOVIE has no account and no developer-operated content server. The developer does not collect your photos, videos, film projects, daily fragments, or usage analytics through the app. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Media and local storage

When you choose photos or videos, the app reads their images, capture times, and approximate locations, and detects whether people appear in them. Selection, analysis, editing, and rendering happen on your iPhone. Projects, daily fragments, preferences, and temporary render files are stored locally. Media available only in iCloud is skipped rather than downloaded for rendering. The app does not upload your media to the developer.

You may clear render caches or delete the app's local projects, daily fragments, and caches in Settings. These actions do not delete originals from Photos. Deleting the app also removes its local data, subject to iOS backup and device settings.

Apple services and your choices

The optional one-time Full Cut purchase, product-price lookup, and purchase restoration use Apple's App Store and may require a network connection. Apple handles purchase and account information under its own privacy policy; the developer does not run a receipt server. Opening these legal pages requires a network connection. Your Photos library and iCloud availability are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Saving a film to Photos or sharing it through the iOS share sheet happens only when you choose that action. Once shared, the recipient or selected service handles that copy under its own terms. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send in that message. Please do not send private photos or videos unless needed for your request.

中文

ME: THE MOVIE（主演：我）无需注册账号，也没有开发者运营的内容服务器。开发者不会通过 App 收集你的照片、视频、影片项目、每天一格内容或使用分析数据。App 不含广告或第三方分析 SDK。

素材与本地存储

你选择照片或视频后，App 会读取画面、拍摄时间和大致地点，并在这台 iPhone 上识别画面中是否有人。挑选、分析、剪辑和渲染均在本机完成。项目、每天一格、偏好设置和临时渲染文件保存在本机。仅存在于 iCloud 的原片会被跳过，不会为了渲染而下载。App 不会把素材上传给开发者。

你可以在“设置”中清理渲染缓存，或删除 App 保存的本地项目、每天一格和缓存；这些操作不会删除“照片”中的原片。删除 App 也会移除其本地数据，但仍受 iOS 备份和设备设置影响。

Apple 服务与主动操作

可选的“完整版”一次性购买、价格查询及恢复购买使用 Apple App Store，可能需要联网。购买与账号信息由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理；开发者不运营收据服务器。打开这些法务网页也需要联网。照片图库和 iCloud 可用性由 Apple 服务及你的设备设置管理。

只有你主动选择，影片才会保存到“照片”或经 iOS 分享菜单发出。分享后的副本由接收者或所选服务按其条款处理。若你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你主动写入邮件的信息；除非解决问题确有必要，请勿发送私人照片或视频。
