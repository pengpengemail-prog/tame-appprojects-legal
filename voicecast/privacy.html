Privacy Policy — VoiceCast

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

  • TAME VoiceCast has no account and no custom server that stores your scripts or videos.
  • Scripts you write and clips you save stay under your control, on your device or in your Photos library.
  • The app shows no ads, collects no analytics, and does not track you.

What we store

Teleprompter scripts and related settings are stored locally on your device. When you choose to save a recording, the video is added to your Photos library. We do not operate a server that receives this content.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

The front camera and microphone are used only when you record or use voice-paced prompting. Photos access is requested in add-only mode so a finished clip can be saved to your library; the app does not browse your existing photo library.

Speech recognition

If you use voice-paced scrolling, speech recognition is provided through Apple's speech APIs. Whether audio is processed on the device or with Apple depends on the system and device capabilities. VoiceCast does not send that audio to a developer server. Voice-paced scrolling is not a paid feature.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple StoreKit.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal profiles, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we do not host your scripts or videos.

Support email

If you email support, your message contains only the details you choose to include. We use it solely to help you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version published with the app.
