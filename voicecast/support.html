FAQ

Where are my scripts and videos stored? Scripts stay on your device. Recordings you save go to your Photos library. There is no VoiceCast account or cloud library.

Why does the app need the microphone or speech recognition? So the script can scroll with your speaking pace. This is not a paid feature. Recognition uses Apple's speech APIs.

Can I record in 4K? The free tier records at 720p. The full version requests 4K on the front camera only when your device supports that preset; otherwise it stays on a supported lower preset.

What is the free script limit? Each script is limited to 300 character or word units on the free tier. A paid plan (weekly, yearly with a 3-day trial, or a one-time purchase) removes that cap.

Do I need to grant full Photo Library access? No. The app only asks to add new clips to Photos.
