TAME Payday Runway Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

TAME Payday Runway is a local paycheck planning calculator. The app does not require an account and does not connect to banks, payment accounts, contacts, location, photos, or other device services.

Information stored in the app

The paycheck amount, payday, buffer, scheduled bills, and manual expenses you enter are stored locally on your device so the app can restore your runway after relaunch. We do not receive or store this information on a server.

Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase entitlement is checked through Apple's StoreKit services.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information, use advertising identifiers, track you across apps or websites, or include third-party analytics.

Children

The app is not designed to collect information from children, and it has no account or messaging features.
