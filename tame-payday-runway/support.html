TAME Payday Runway Support

Payday Runway is a local paycheck planning calculator. It does not connect to banks or move money.

Common questions

How do I restore my unlock?

Open the unlock screen from cycle settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Where is my data?

Your cycle, bills, and manual expenses are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Why did my safe-to-spend number change?

The number subtracts the bills, buffer, and manual expenses currently entered for the cycle. Check cycle settings if a value is not what you expect.
