Privacy Policy — FocusLock

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

  • TAME FocusLock has no account and no custom server.
  • Your selected apps, categories, websites, templates, and session history are stored locally on your device.
  • The app shows no ads, collects no analytics, and does not track you.

What we store

Shield selections, rule templates, and focus-session statistics stay in the app's local storage. This data is included only in your own device backups; it is never sent to us.

Screen management

To block apps, categories, and websites during a session, FocusLock uses Apple's Family Controls and related system APIs, including the system picker. Apple provides those controls on your device. We do not receive a list of your apps on a developer server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple StoreKit.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal profiles, advertising identifiers, location, or usage analytics, and we operate no server that receives your shield lists or session history.

Support email

If you email support, your message contains only the details you choose to include. We use it solely to help you and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version published with the app.
