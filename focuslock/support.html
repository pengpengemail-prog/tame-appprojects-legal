FAQ

Where is my data stored? Selections, templates, and session history stay on your device. FocusLock has no account and no cloud server.

Why do I have to authorize screen management? Blocking apps, categories, and websites uses Apple's system Family Controls APIs. The app cannot shield items until you grant that authorization in the system prompt.

What is free and what is paid? The free tier allows 2 shield items (apps, categories, and websites combined) and 2 templates, plus timed sessions, optional strict mode, and a 7-day chart. A paid plan (weekly, yearly with a 3-day trial, or a one-time purchase) removes those two caps. Strict mode is currently free.

Can I schedule a lock for later? Not in this version. Saved rules are templates you start yourself. The app does not automatically lock at a clock time.

What if authorization is denied? Open iOS Settings and allow screen management for FocusLock, then return to the app. The picker will not open until authorization is granted.
